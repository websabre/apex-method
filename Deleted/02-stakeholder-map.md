# RideFlow Stakeholder Map

## Purpose

Identify every stakeholder that influences the RideFlow platform.

Understanding stakeholders is one of the first responsibilities of a Solution Architect because architecture exists to satisfy business needs, not technical preferences.

---

# Primary Stakeholders

## Riders

Goals

- Fast booking
- Affordable pricing
- Safe rides
- Reliable ETA

Measures of Success

- Short wait time
- High availability
- Accurate pricing

---

## Drivers

Goals

- Maximize earnings
- Fair ride allocation
- Simple onboarding
- Fast payouts

Measures of Success

- Monthly income
- Daily completed rides
- Driver retention

---

## Fleet Owners

Goals

- Vehicle utilization
- Driver management
- Revenue visibility

---

# Internal Stakeholders

## Customer Support

Needs

- Ride history
- Driver details
- Payment records

---

## Finance

Needs

- Revenue reports
- GST reports
- Subscription management
- Payment reconciliation

---

## Operations

Needs

- Live dashboards
- Incident monitoring
- City-level metrics

---

## Marketing

Needs

- Campaign management
- Coupons
- Customer segmentation

---

## Engineering

Needs

- Stable APIs
- CI/CD
- Observability
- Architecture documentation

---

# Architect's Lens

Question

Which stakeholders generate the most non-functional requirements?

Reflection

Many architectural failures occur because stakeholder requirements were never identified.

Architecture begins with people, not software.
