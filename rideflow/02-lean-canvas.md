# RideFlow Lean Canvas

## Problem

Drivers lose a significant portion of their earnings due to high commission fees.

Riders often experience unpredictable pricing, inconsistent availability, and limited transparency.

Traditional ride-hailing platforms are expensive to operate and difficult to evolve.

---

## Customer Segments

### Primary

- Bike Taxi Drivers
- Riders

### Secondary

- Fleet Owners
- Small Transport Operators

### Future

- Logistics Companies
- Corporate Mobility Programs
- Delivery Partners

---

## Unique Value Proposition

"A driver-first mobility platform where drivers keep more of what they earn, riders receive transparent pricing, and the platform evolves through modern cloud-native engineering."

---

## Solution

- Subscription model for drivers
- AI-assisted ride matching
- Dynamic but transparent pricing
- ONDC integration
- Digital wallet
- Fleet management
- Real-time analytics

## Unfair Advantage

RideFlow is designed around a sustainable driver-first business model supported by cloud-native architecture, AI-driven operations, and open ecosystem integration rather than commission maximization.
